# Leadership

## Goal
Executive communication, prioritization, roadmap, metrics and stakeholder negotiation.

## Architect workflow
1. Establish business context.
2. Identify current state.
3. Identify risks and constraints.
4. Define target state.
5. Compare options.
6. Select controls and architecture.
7. Define roadmap and ownership.
8. Define measurable outcomes.

## Deliverables
Diagram, decision record, risks, assumptions, roadmap, controls and executive summary.

## Instructor
Ollama acts as a skeptical architecture review board. It asks for justification and challenges weak assumptions.
