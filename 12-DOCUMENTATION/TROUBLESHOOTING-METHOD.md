# TROUBLESHOOTING METHOD

Use the same process throughout all four years:

1. Clarify the business impact.
2. Identify affected user, device, application and identity.
3. Establish scope.
4. Reproduce safely if possible.
5. Gather evidence.
6. Form hypotheses.
7. Test the highest-value hypothesis.
8. Change one thing at a time.
9. Validate.
10. Document root cause and preventive action.

## Identity-specific questions

- Who is the identity?
- Is the account active?
- How is authentication occurring?
- What system is authoritative?
- What entitlement is being requested?
- Who owns that entitlement?
- Is the access appropriate?
- Is it least privilege?
- Is there evidence of approval?
- What logs support the conclusion?
