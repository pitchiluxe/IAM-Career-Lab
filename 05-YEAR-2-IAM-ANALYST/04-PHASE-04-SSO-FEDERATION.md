# SSO & Federation

## Goal
SAML, OIDC, OAuth concepts, IdP/SP roles, claims, trust relationships.

## Scenario
OMARI Technologies is moving from traditional help desk operations toward centralized identity governance.

## Workflow
1. Identify the identity/business object.
2. Identify owner and requested entitlement.
3. Verify authorization.
4. Investigate evidence.
5. Apply least privilege.
6. Validate.
7. Document for audit.

## Instructor
Ollama provides hints progressively and withholds the root cause.

## Acceptance
Explain authentication, authorization, identity lifecycle state, and business justification involved.
